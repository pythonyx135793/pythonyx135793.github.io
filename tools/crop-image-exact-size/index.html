Crop an image to exact size

The focal sliders control which part of the source remains when its proportions differ from the requested output. Processing happens in your browser; selected file contents are not uploaded.

Choose an image, output dimensions, and focal position.

How to use this tool safely

  1. Enter the required output width and height, then choose the source image.
  2. Move the horizontal and vertical focal controls toward the subject that must remain visible.
  3. Create the crop, verify its pixel dimensions, and inspect headroom, borders, and document edges.

Input, output, and compatibility

JPG, PNG, and WebP inputs are supported and output is PNG. The crop fills the requested aspect ratio by removing outer content; it does not add padding or identify faces automatically.

What Readyvio verifies

Readyvio verifies the exported pixel dimensions. It does not assess biometric rules, official photo composition, safe areas, or whether important content was cropped out.

Common questions

Why are parts of my image removed?

Exact cropping must remove content whenever the source and target aspect ratios differ. Adjust the focal controls or choose a padded editor if nothing can be removed.

Can this make an official passport photo?

It can produce specified pixel dimensions, but it cannot certify pose, lighting, background, head size, or current government requirements.

Before you submit

This is a focal-position crop, not biometric or authority approval. Check headroom, face size, and current official requirements yourself.

Readyvio is not affiliated with an issuing authority and cannot guarantee acceptance. Check the destination's current rules and keep the original file.
